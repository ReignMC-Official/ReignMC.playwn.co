The Law of the Reign

Server Rules

Every nation needs a foundation. This is ours — read it before you claim your first plot.

I. General Conduct II. Building & Claims III. Towny & Nations IV. PvP & Raiding V. Chat & Communication VI. Economy & Trading VII. Punishment Tiers
Section I

General Conduct

  • No hacked clients or exploits. X-ray texture packs, kill-aura, fly hacks, and duplication bugs are bannable regardless of intent.
  • No harassment or hate speech. Zero tolerance for slurs, targeted harassment, or discriminatory content in any channel — in-game or Discord.
  • No real-world doxxing or threats. Sharing someone's personal information or threatening real-world harm results in an immediate permanent ban.
  • Respect staff decisions. Appeals go through the proper channel — arguing with staff in public chat won't overturn anything.
  • One account per player unless a staff member has approved an alt for a specific reason.
Staff reserve the right to act on conduct that damages the community even if it isn't explicitly listed here.
Section II

Building & Claims

  • Claim before you build. Unclaimed builds outside town limits can be broken down without warning.
  • No griefing inside claims. If Towny protection is blocking you, that's the system working as intended — find another plot.
  • No offensive builds. Structures depicting hate symbols, slurs, or explicit content will be removed and may result in a ban.
  • Lag-inducing builds (large redstone farms, entity-heavy contraptions) may be asked to be modified or relocated if they impact server performance.
  • Abandoned claims (no login for 60+ days) may be reclaimed by the town or released back to the wild.
Section III

Towny & Nations

  • Mayors are responsible for their town's residents. Repeated rule violations by residents reflect on town standing.
  • Nation wars must be declared through the proper Towny war mechanic — no sneak-attacking allies mid-truce.
  • No exploiting town claim mechanics to surround or lock out another town from expansion unfairly.
  • Diplomacy is binding. Alliances and treaties formed in-game are expected to be honored; breaking one isn't a bannable offense, but it's fair game for other nations to respond to in-character.
Section IV

PvP & Raiding

  • PvP is enabled outside of spawn and designated safe zones (Elysian is always neutral ground).
  • Raiding requires an active war declaration against the target nation — raiding without one is treated as griefing.
  • No spawn killing or safe-zone camping to bait players out of protected areas.
  • Combat logging (disconnecting to avoid a fight) may result in a temporary PvP-related restriction.
Section V

Chat & Communication

  • English only in global chat so staff can moderate effectively; other languages are fine in DMs or town chat.
  • No spamming, advertising, or excessive caps.
  • No impersonating staff or claiming false authority in chat.
  • Keep political and religious debate out of public channels — take it to DMs if you want to discuss it.
Section VI

Economy & Trading

  • No real-money trading for in-game currency, items, or claims outside of the official store.
  • Scamming another player in a trade is bannable if proven (screenshots/logs required for a report).
  • Duping items or currency through any bug is an instant permanent ban, no warning.
  • Shop price manipulation designed to exploit market mechanics may be reversed by staff.
Section VII

Punishment Tiers

Punishments scale with severity and history. Staff may skip tiers for serious first offenses (hacking, doxxing, duping).

TierExampleTypical Action
Tier 1Spam, minor chat disrespect, caps abuseWarning → short mute
Tier 2Repeated Tier 1, minor griefing, unclaimed build destructionTemp mute/ban, restitution
Tier 3Hacked clients, duping, doxxing, hate speechPermanent ban
