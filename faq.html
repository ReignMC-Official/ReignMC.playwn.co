Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what new players ask most, before their first night on Aegrath.

ReignMC runs on Paper 1.21.x for Java players, with Bedrock support via Geyser and Floodgate — so both Java and Bedrock players can join the same world.
The server is open to everyone — no whitelist required, just connect and start playing.
Towny lets you found a town with /town new once you've got enough in-game currency, or join an existing one if a mayor invites you. Full commands are covered in-game via /towny help.
PvP is not restricted outside of spawn. The one exception: players under 10 hours of playtime can't be attacked unless they've started a war themselves — so brand-new players get a grace period to get settled.
The full story of the First Sundering unlocks once the server hits a set active-player threshold. Until then, only fragments surface through in-world events.
Not at all — store packages are either cosmetics or support ranks. Nothing sold gives a gameplay or combat advantage.
Bug reports go through the bug bounty program in Discord; rule violations should be reported to staff with evidence (screenshots or coordinates) in a support ticket.
