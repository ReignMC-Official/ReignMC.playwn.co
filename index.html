The Shattered Realm

Nine Continents, One Fracture

Every biome on Aegrath still carries the scar of the Sundering — climates thrown together, ley lines exposed, borders that no longer make sense. That's where your nation begins.

I

Frostheim

Glacial highlands where the cold itself feels deliberate, like the world is still flinching.

II

Verdantium

Overgrown lowlands and river valleys — the most contested farmland on the map.

III

Ashenwaste

Scorched flats and cracked earth, closest to where the rift first tore open.

IV

Aurelian Deserts

Golden dunes hiding buried ruins from before the Sundering split the world apart.

V

Stonehelm

Mountain holds and fortress towns, built by nations who trust walls over words.

VI

Corvus Reach

Windswept coastlines and cliffside settlements watching the horizon for what comes next.

VII

Zaharin Jungles

Dense, humid, and largely unmapped — the frontier for nations still expanding.

VIII

Morgrim Marshes

Wetlands thick with fog and older magic than most players ever go looking for.

IX

Elysian

The neutral spawn realm — untouched ground where every reign begins equal.
